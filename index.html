How To Participate

Each company can submit up to 100 bids during the auction. Every bid must be at least $1M higher than the previous highest bid and exceed the starting price of $325M. To place a bid, utilize a one-time password from your list. While these passwords are linked to your identity internally, your identity remains anonymous to others. In this auction, you pay the amount you bid. By placing a bid, you affirm (using your credentials) your commitment to pay that amount if you win. After a bid, the one-time password is invalidated and becomes visible to others, yet your identity stays protected.
